OUR STORY

From The Orchard To Your Doorstep

Tumanza was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to pure, organic, and sustainably sourced nuts. We partner directly with small farmers across the globe to bring you the freshest harvest.

No preservatives, no artificial flavors, no compromise. Just nature at its peak — cracked, roasted, and packed with care for you and your family.

How It All Began

Tumanza started in 2018 with a small family farm in the highlands of Gilgit-Baltistan. What began as a passion for sharing the pure, untouched flavors of mountain-grown nuts quickly grew into a mission to connect health-conscious families with nature's finest organic produce.

Our founders spent years building relationships with local farmers who share our commitment to organic, chemical-free farming. Every product we sell can be traced back to the farm it came from — that's our promise of transparency and trust.

Today, Tumanza serves thousands of families across Pakistan and beyond, delivering handpicked, lab-tested organic nuts, seeds, herbs, and teas straight to their doorsteps. We believe that good food should be accessible, honest, and kind to the planet.
